anglemap.py#

Deprecated since version 2025: The functions in this file were originally used for analysis of Optris images, in particular for lambertian and occlusion corrections. However, due to the high number of necessary and redundant parameters, these functions have been refactored to use OOP principals. Thus, for any new analysis, the TemperatureSet, CameraPosition, and ROI classes should be used instead of the functions in this file.

See also

temperatureimage.TemperatureSet

camera.CameraPosition

optrisROI.ROI

Temperature Helper Functions#

anglemap.cameraChipVector(x, y, rho=0, phi=0, view_coords=False)[source]#

Finds the vector from a point on the chip (defined by x and y coordinates) to a point on the camera lens (defined by rho and phi)

anglemap.apertureRadii(x, y, rho=0, phi=0, view_coords=False)[source]#

Determines the distance from the center(s) of the lid aperture(s) to the intersection of the camera chip vector and the aperture(s)’s plane

Temperature Calculation Functions#

anglemap.pointTemp(x, y, maxTemp, rho=0, phi=0)[source]#

Simulates lambertian and occlusion distortion of a temperature measurement for a point on the chip and a single point on the camera lens.

anglemap.approxTemp(x, y, maxTemp, resolution=20)[source]#

Simulates lambertian and occlusion distortion of a temperature measurement for a point on the chip over the entire camera lens. Resolution determines how many points on the camera lens will be used for the approximation.

Note

Point selection on the camera lens uses an algorithm that scales with resolution cubed (rather than squared), and biases towards outer points.

anglemap.correctedTemp(x, y, temperature, resolution=2, lambertian=True, occlusion=True, quadratic=True)[source]#

Gives an approximate correction (lambertian and/or occlusion) for a temperature measurement for a point on the chip over the entire camera lens.

Image Functions#

Returns the corners of a rectangular ROI. roi must be either a tuple of at least four xy-coordinates or one of ‘chip’, ‘brick’, and ‘fullbrick’

Returns true if a point (defined with x and y coordinates) lies on the perimeter of a rectangle (defined by its top_left and bottom_right corners)

anglemap.inRoi(x, y, top_left, bottom_right)[source]#

anglemap.inRoiPoly(x, y, roi)[source]#

Holistic Correction Helper Functions#

anglemap.imageCorrectRuntimeEstimate(roi, correction_resolution, lambertian, occlusion, quadratic, cmap)[source]#

Gives an approximate runtime for temperature correction. Runtime scales linearly with number of pixels (temperatures) and quadratically (or sometimes cubically) with the correction_resolution.
